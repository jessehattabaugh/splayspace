# SplaySpace

An infinite flat world to wander with your friends.

## goals

-   serverless for massive worldwide scaling
-   one shared practically infinite world
-   simple and fun raster art editor
-   scarcity based resource collection economy
-   procedurally generated terrain
-   runs on feature phones, smart phones, tablets, and desktop (switch?)
-   chat and share designs and resources
-   gamepad/keyboard/touch/mouse support
-   limited ownership window upgradable with monetization
-   easy to copy each other's work to encourage creative evolution

Imagine Minecraft meets The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past. It's a 2D top down world you can explore with your friends. You can build and edit the world together. You can collect resources and trade them with each other. You can fight monsters and solve puzzles. You can chat and share your creations. You can play with a gamepad, keyboard, touch, or mouse. You can play on your feature phone, smart phone, tablet, or desktop. You can play for free or pay to own a piece of the world. You can copy each other's work to encourage creative evolution. You can play in a world that is practically infinite. You can play in a world that is always online. You can play in a world that is always changing. You can play in a world that is always growing. You can play in a world that is always fun. You can play in a world that is always yours.
